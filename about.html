Which areas do you cover?

We have offices in Darlington, Newcastle, Northallerton, Carlisle, Penrith and Kendal, and cover the towns around them. See our Areas we cover page for the full list.

Do you handle both sales and lettings?

Yes. We handle residential sales and lettings, as well as student accommodation and HMO property management.

Is the valuation free?

Yes. Your valuation or rental appraisal is free and there is no obligation to instruct us afterwards.
