Landlord questions

The compliance basics.

General guidance only, not legal advice. Rules change, so we keep you up to date.

Is the management review free?

Yes. It is free and there is no obligation to instruct us afterwards.

What safety checks do landlords need?

In England this typically includes an annual gas safety check where there is gas, an electrical installation report at least every five years, working smoke alarms on each floor and carbon monoxide alarms where required, and a valid EPC.

What about the tenant’s deposit?

If you take a tenancy deposit, it generally has to be protected in a government-approved scheme within 30 days and the tenant given the prescribed information. We handle this for the properties we manage.

What are right to rent checks?

Landlords in England must check that adult tenants have the right to rent before the tenancy starts. We carry out these checks as part of our referencing.

Do the Renters’ Rights changes affect me?

Tenancy law in England is changing. We keep the landlords we work with informed of what applies to them and when. Speak to your local expert for the latest position.

Can you manage a portfolio or an HMO?

Yes. We look after single lets, larger portfolios, HMOs and student lets.
