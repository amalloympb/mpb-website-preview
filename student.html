For landlords

Letting to students? Know the basics.

A few things worth knowing before you let a property to students.

Tenancies and guarantors

Students often rent as a group. A joint tenancy makes everyone responsible for the whole rent, and many landlords ask for a guarantor.

Plan around the academic year

Student lets often follow the academic year, so it pays to start early.

HMO rules

A house let to three or more sharing students may be an HMO, with extra licensing and safety rules.

HMO landlords

Questions

Before you decide.

Straight answers to what students and landlords ask us most.

Are student properties HMOs?

Often, yes. A property let to three or more students sharing facilities may be an HMO, so licensing and safety rules can apply. See our HMO page for more.

How is student rent shown?

Student rents are usually shown per person per week (pppw). Our property search shows this where the listing provides it.

Is the rental appraisal free?

Yes. Your rental appraisal is free and there is no obligation to instruct us afterwards.

Is this legal advice?

General guidance for landlords in England, not legal advice. Check the rules that apply to your property with your local council or a qualified adviser.
