Questions

Before you decide.

Straight answers to what vendors ask us most.

Is the valuation really free?

Yes. Your valuation is free and there is no obligation to instruct us afterwards.

How long does it take to sell?

It depends on the price, the condition of the property and the local market. At your valuation your expert will give you a realistic timeline for your home.

How much will it cost to sell?

Fees depend on the service and your property. You will get a clear written quote after your valuation, so there are no surprises. You can also estimate your costs with our calculator.

Will my home be on Rightmove?

Yes. We market your home on Rightmove and on our own property search, with 360° virtual tours where available.

Do you cover my area?

We have seven offices across the North East, North Yorkshire and Cumbria. Enter your postcode above and we will match you with the local team.

What happens to my details?

We only use them to contact you about your request, as set out in our Privacy Policy. We never sell your data.
