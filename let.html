Questions

Before you decide.

Straight answers to what landlords ask us most.

Is the rental appraisal really free?

Yes. Your appraisal is free and there is no obligation to instruct us afterwards.

How do you decide the rent?

Your expert looks at your property, its location and the demand from tenants in your area, then recommends a rent and the best way to market it.

What does tenant referencing involve?

Referencing typically covers identity, right to rent, income and previous landlord references. Your expert will explain exactly what we check for your property.

What do I need to have in place before letting?

In England that usually includes a valid EPC, a gas safety certificate if there is gas, an electrical safety report, working smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, and the tenant’s right to rent checks. We will talk you through what applies to you. This is general guidance, not legal advice.

Can you manage the property after it is let?

Yes. We can look after maintenance, compliance and day-to-day communication. See our landlord services.

Do you let HMOs and student properties?

Yes. We have specialist teams for HMOs and student lets.
